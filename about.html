Biography & Passion

Bridging Core Computing & Creative Storytelling

I am Abdul Rafay Shaikh (also known as Shaikh Muhammad Rafay), a dedicated Computer Science educator and BS–IT scholar (2nd Year) at Government College University, Hyderabad (GCUH).

My teaching approach is rooted in clarity, structured lesson delivery, and empathy for students tackling technical concepts for the first time. For Grade 9 students, subjects like binary number conversions, logic gates, algorithm flowcharts, and network architectures can feel intimidating when taught dryly. My mission is to demystify computing through interactive examples, visual worksheets, and hands-on practice that build genuine confidence for school exams and board certifications.

Alongside education, I am an intermediate video editor proficient in Adobe Premiere Pro. Video editing gives me the power to structure pacing, emphasize key takeaways, and produce cinematic educational lectures and vertical reels that hook viewers from the very first frame.
