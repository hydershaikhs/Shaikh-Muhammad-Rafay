Teaching Philosophy & Story

I am Abdul Rafay Shaikh (also known as Shaikh Muhammad Rafay), a Computer Science educator for Grade 9 and a 2nd-year BS–IT scholar at Government College University, Hyderabad (GCUH).

My teaching approach breaks down abstract computing topics—number systems, binary conversions, memory hierarchies, logic gates, algorithm flowcharts, and network architectures—into intuitive, visual lessons that prepare students thoroughly for board examinations.

Alongside teaching, I am an intermediate video editor proficient in Adobe Premiere Pro. Video post-production allows me to structure pacing, emphasize key concepts, and deliver high-engagement tutorials and vertical reels that hook viewers from the very first frame.
